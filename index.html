02 — THE IDEA

Movement is only
half the story.

↗

Humanoids have learned how to move.
Now, how do they interact?

Beautiful whole-body motion is not the same as reliable physical interaction. The difficult moments happen at contact: approaching an object, securing it, keeping hold, adapting to changes, and letting go.

We envision interaction as a native capability of humanoid robots—so people can guide movement without having to micromanage every physical contact. LIFe is one step in that direction.
